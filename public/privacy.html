Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 May 2026

Dearly helps you turn a written note into something worth sending — a letter, a voice note, a small piece of you. Because what people write here is often personal, we keep our data practices simple and honest. This page explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and how to get it deleted.

Who runs Dearly. Dearly is operated by Bhanu Kowshik Papineni, an individual based in Oakland, California, USA. For any privacy question or request, email hello@dearlynotes.app.

What we collect

We only collect what's needed to create and deliver your letters. That includes:

  • The content you create — the text of your letter, the sender and recipient names you enter, and any styling, stickers, or layout choices.
  • Voice recordings you add to a letter.
  • Images and GIFs you upload into a letter.
  • Recipient email addresses, if you choose to send a letter by email.
  • Feedback you voluntarily submit (a star rating and optional text).
  • Basic usage analytics — pages viewed and general interactions, collected only if you accept analytics cookies (see "Cookies & analytics" below).

We do not ask you to create an account, and we don't collect names, passwords, or payment details for sign-up, because there is no sign-up.

How we use it

  • To build your letter and make it viewable by the person you share it with.
  • To store voice notes and images so they still work when the recipient opens the link on another device.
  • To send a letter by email, when you ask us to.
  • To generate a suggested email subject line for letters sent by email.
  • To convert text to speech, when you use that feature.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how Dearly is used so we can improve it (only with your consent).

We do not sell your personal data, and we don't use your letters to advertise to you.

We use a small number of trusted service providers ("subprocessors") to run Dearly. Your data passes through them only to deliver the features above:

Each provider processes data under its own privacy terms. We share only what each one needs to do its job.

Shared letters are accessible by link

When you share a letter — by link, QR code, or email — anyone who has that link can open it, along with any voice notes or images in it. Shared content is reachable by its link and is not protected by a password. Please only share letters with people you trust, and only send to recipients who expect to hear from you.

Cookies & analytics

Dearly does not load any analytics or tracking cookies until you accept them. When you first visit, you'll see a short banner. If you accept, we load Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to measure aggregate usage and record anonymised session replays (mouse movement, clicks, and scrolling — never your keystrokes) so we can see where the experience can be improved. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set and no session is recorded. You can change your mind by clearing your browser storage for this site, which brings the banner back.

How long we keep your data

We keep your letters, voice notes, images, and any recipient email you entered until you ask us to delete them. We don't currently auto-expire shared letters. If you'd like something removed, email us (see below) and we'll take care of it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • Delete a specific letter, voice note, image, or any personal data we hold.
  • Access a copy of the data we have about you.
  • Correct information that's wrong.

To make any of these requests, email hello@dearlynotes.app. We'll respond within 30 days. Because Dearly has no accounts, please send your request from, or include, the email address or link tied to the content so we can find it.

If you're a California resident, you have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know what we collect, the right to delete, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We do not sell or "share" personal information as those terms are defined under California law. Use the same email above to exercise these rights.

Children

Dearly is not intended for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has used Dearly, email us and we'll delete the related data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Dearly grows. When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top. Significant changes will be made clear within the app where practical.

Contact

Questions, requests, or concerns? Email hello@dearlynotes.app.
